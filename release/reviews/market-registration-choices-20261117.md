The table has RLS and no client grants or policies. This records the submitted declaration; it does not purport to make unreviewed legal texts legally sufficient or to enforce acceptance for every existing shared-account route. Existing legal-document pilot/review limitations are preserved.

Previously seen copies cannot be recalled. The existing per-listing choice remains a separate voluntary decision; editing an old listing cannot silently change the global chat-sharing preference.
