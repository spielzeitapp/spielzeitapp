# Explicit completion, archive and permanent deletion — TEST and LIVE

Adds an owner-only completion dialog for sold/gifted/swapped/team/withdrawn outcomes, then Archive or Permanent Delete, with a separate required final confirmation. Drafts can only be withdrawn. Existing team pledge locks the selected outcome and is retained without another pledge. New team completion announces the seller contribution atomically, without increasing balance. A missing or stale revision is rejected. No buyer response is required.

Archived listings have status Vergeben and archived_at, disappear from public catalog (existing server-side available/reserved filter), and appear under My Listings / Done. Archived listings cannot be republished and their UI offers permanent deletion. Archived photos/chats remain available for traceability until actual deletion. Permanent removal deletes the listing; existing FK cascades delete threads/messages and saved references. Pending/confirmed cash snapshots use ON DELETE SET NULL and remain confirmable/booked. Photo removal uses the owner-authorized Storage API after deletion; failures are explicitly reported and queued per account in localStorage for retry on the next market visit. No Storage SQL deletion is used. This retry is local to the browser/account and is not a guaranteed server-side cleanup job.

Outcome records store only random record UUID, listing UUID while the listing exists, club UUID and outcome. On listing deletion the listing UUID becomes NULL; no user ID, name, title, photo, amount or precise timestamp is retained for statistics. Unique listing UUID prevents duplicate counts; retry after deletion never creates a new count. Withdrawal is excluded from success counts. Only market managers may read aggregate sold/gifted/swapped counts. Team payment amounts and confirmed receipts remain exclusively in the scoped cash ledger, not in general marketplace statistics. No historic success is guessed or backfilled.
